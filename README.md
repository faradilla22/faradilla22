### 🚀 About Me

I'm an Informatics Engineering graduate from Universitas Sriwijaya (GPA 3.95/4.00), specializing in data analysis, machine learning, and UI/UX. I turn messy data into clear dashboards, predictive models, and actionable insights, from a hybrid model that lifted classification accuracy from 82% to 92% to a case study of cohort analysis of 460K+ transactions that pinpointed where customers drop off.  
I bring hands-on IT and business exposure: I developed the role-based access backend for an Equipment Criticality Rating module at PT Pupuk Sriwidjaja Palembang (100% test cases accepted) and reconciled marketplace sales data in Accurate ERP at PT Sahabat Anabul Indonesia . Beyond the classroom, I served as liaison with external partners, chaired plenary sessions, and mentored 20+ new students in programming at HMIF Unsri, and I delivered an end-to-end UI/UX project, from research to usability testing, at Google Developer Student Clubs .  
These experiences sharpened my ability to work with data accurately, communicate findings clearly, and coordinate across teams to keep projects and operations running smoothly. I'm drawn to Data Analyst, BI Analyst, and business support roles where I can build and maintain reliable reporting systems, turn raw data into decisions teams can trust, and provide the digital groundwork that keeps larger initiatives moving forward.
